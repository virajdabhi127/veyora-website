Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 13 October 2026

You can request deletion of your personal data and account information from Veyora at any time. This page explains what gets deleted and how to request it.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, phone number)
  • Linked device data and historical load/usage records
  • WhatsApp opt-in status and associated message history with our business number
  • Any stored preferences or settings tied to your account

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to virajdabhi910@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request", including:

  1. The email address or phone number associated with your Veyora account
  2. Confirmation that you want your account and associated data permanently deleted
Request Deletion via Email

What Happens Next

We will verify your request and process deletion within 30 days. You'll receive a confirmation email once your data has been removed from our active systems. Some information may be retained briefly in backups or as required by law, after which it is also purged.

WhatsApp-Specific Data

If you only want to stop receiving WhatsApp messages without deleting your full account, reply "STOP" to any message from our business number, or email us and we'll remove your WhatsApp opt-in while keeping your dashboard account active.

Contact

Questions about this process? Reach us at virajdabhi910@gmail.com.
