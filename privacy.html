Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Veyora ("we," "our," "us") provides smart energy monitoring hardware and software, including devices, a web dashboard, and WhatsApp-based notifications. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and password (hashed) when you register.
  • Device data: readings from your Veyora energy monitoring device(s), such as power usage, voltage, load history, and device status (online/offline).
  • WhatsApp data: your WhatsApp number, if you opt in to receive alerts or summaries, and the content of messages you send to our WhatsApp business number.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and basic usage logs for security and debugging.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate your account and dashboard
  • To send you energy usage alerts, summaries, and service notifications (including via WhatsApp)
  • To monitor and improve device performance and reliability
  • To provide customer support when you contact us
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. WhatsApp Messaging

If you interact with our WhatsApp Business number, we process your phone number and message content solely to respond to you and deliver the notifications you've opted into. We do not use this data for advertising or share it with third parties for marketing.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with:

  • Service providers who help us operate (e.g., cloud hosting, messaging infrastructure such as Meta/WhatsApp)
  • Authorities, where required by law

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and device data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion at any time — see our Data Deletion page.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and access controls. No system is completely secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.
