FAQ

Questions people ask first

Does it replace my DISCOM meter?

No. Your utility meter stays and remains the official billing meter. Veyora sits alongside it and gives you the detail the utility meter does not.

How accurate is it?

Every Veyora unit is carefully calibrated for voltage and current against a reference instrument before it leaves our bench, and we verify it again on site after installation. On our first field installation, the unit's kWh reading matched the PGVCL meter on a live household load. Veyora is built to give you dependable, day-to-day usage and cost insight. Your DISCOM meter remains the official meter for billing. (Note: Veyora energy monitor can have error margins which would typically lie within ± 1 %)

What if my WiFi or internet goes down?

The unit keeps measuring and showing readings on its own display. It stores data on the device and uploads it once the connection is back.

Is the installation safe?

Installation is done by Veyora, with a skilled electrician.

Can it monitor my AC or geyser separately?

Not directly, but you can get the idea by monitoring its parent circuit.

Where is my data stored?

Readings are sent to the Veyora cloud and shown when you login the dashboard.
